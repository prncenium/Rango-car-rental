# CAR RENTAL

## SAFETY RULES, GUIDELINES & TERMS AND CONDITIONS

Please read carefully before accepting the vehicle.

Your safety and the safety of other road users are our priority. By accepting the vehicle, the customer agrees to follow all applicable traffic laws, safety guidelines, and the terms and conditions below.

### 1. SECURITY DEPOSIT & VEHICLE DAMAGE

A security deposit is required before the vehicle is handed over.
The vehicle will be inspected before and after the rental period.
Any existing scratches, dents, or damage should be reported before taking the vehicle.
The security deposit will be held until the vehicle has been inspected after return.
If new damage, loss, or excessive cleaning is found, the applicable amount may be deducted from the security deposit.
If the total cost of damage or loss is higher than the security deposit, the customer will be responsible for paying the remaining balance.
If there are no outstanding charges or damages, the remaining security deposit will be refunded according to the agreed rental terms.

### 2. ACCIDENTS & VEHICLE REPAIR

In case of an accident:
Stop the vehicle safely and check whether anyone needs medical assistance.
Contact emergency services if required.
Inform the rental company as soon as possible.
Do not leave the accident scene unless it is necessary for safety or required by authorities.
Do not admit liability or make private settlements on behalf of the rental company without authorization.
Provide all necessary information and cooperate with the rental company and relevant authorities.

If the vehicle is damaged and requires repair:
The customer may be responsible for applicable repair costs arising from their use of the vehicle, subject to the rental agreement and applicable insurance terms.
A daily vehicle downtime charge may apply for each day the vehicle remains unavailable for rental due to repairs resulting from the customer's use of the vehicle.
Towing, recovery, insurance excess/deductibles, or other applicable costs may also be charged where permitted under the rental agreement.

### 3. SPEED LIMIT & OVER-SPEEDING

Customers must follow all legally applicable speed limits and any additional speed limit communicated by the rental company.
Dangerous or excessive speeding is strictly prohibited.
For repeated over-speeding, the customer may receive warnings.
Three warnings may be issued for repeated over-speeding.
If the customer continues to exceed the permitted speed after the warnings, an applicable penalty/fine may be charged.
Serious or dangerous speeding may result in immediate termination of the rental agreement.
Please remember: Arriving safely is more important than arriving quickly.

### 4. TRAFFIC RULES & FINES

The customer must follow all traffic laws, including:
Traffic signals and road signs.
Speed limits.
Seat-belt requirements.
Parking restrictions.
Lane and overtaking rules.
Mobile-phone restrictions while driving.
Any applicable toll, permit, or road regulations.

Any traffic fine, challan, parking penalty, towing charge, or other penalty caused by the customer's actions during the rental period will be the customer's responsibility, subject to applicable law and the rental agreement.

### 5. SEAT BELTS ARE COMPULSORY

The driver and all passengers must wear seat belts whenever required by law.
The driver must not allow passengers to travel in unsafe positions or outside designated seats.
Never exceed the vehicle's permitted passenger capacity.

### 6. NO DRIVING UNDER THE INFLUENCE

Driving under the influence of alcohol, drugs, or any substance that may impair driving ability is strictly prohibited.
If the customer drives while impaired, they may be responsible for any resulting:
Accident or vehicle damage.
Injury or property damage.
Traffic or legal penalties.
Towing or recovery costs.
Insurance-related costs.
Other applicable expenses.
The rental company may terminate the rental immediately in such circumstances.

### 7. NO SMOKING / VAPING

For your safety and for the comfort of future customers, please keep the vehicle smoke-free.
Smoking inside the vehicle is prohibited.
Vaping or using other smoking products inside the vehicle is also prohibited.
Cigarette burns, ash damage, smoke odor, stains, or interior damage may result in additional cleaning or repair charges.
Please help us keep the vehicle clean, fresh, and safe for everyone.

### 8. AUTHORIZED DRIVER ONLY

Only approved drivers listed in the rental agreement may operate the vehicle.
The vehicle must not be given to another person to drive without prior permission from the rental company.
The customer may be responsible for any damage, accident, fine, or loss caused by an unauthorized driver.

### 9. SAFE DRIVING REQUIREMENTS

Customers must:
Drive according to road and weather conditions.
Maintain a safe distance from other vehicles.
Use indicators when turning or changing lanes.
Check mirrors and blind spots before changing lanes.
Avoid sudden or dangerous manoeuvres.
Follow posted road signs.
Avoid aggressive or reckless driving.
Never use the vehicle for racing, drifting, or speed competitions.

### 10. MOBILE PHONE USE

Do not use a mobile phone in a way that distracts you while driving.
If you need to make a call, send a message, or use your phone for navigation, safely stop the vehicle where permitted.
Never text while driving.

### 11. FATIGUE & DROWSY DRIVING

Do not drive when extremely tired, sleepy, or unable to concentrate.
Take regular breaks during long journeys.
If you feel drowsy, stop somewhere safe and rest before continuing.
Never risk your life or someone else's life just to reach your destination faster.

### 12. FUEL

The vehicle will be provided with an agreed fuel level.
The vehicle should be returned with the same fuel level unless otherwise agreed.
If the vehicle is returned with less fuel, the applicable refueling/fuel charge may be deducted or charged to the customer.

### 13. LATE RETURN

The vehicle must be returned at the agreed date and time.
Customers should contact the rental company in advance if they expect to be late.
Additional hourly or daily rental charges may apply for late returns according to the agreed rental terms.
Failure to return the vehicle without communication may be treated as a breach of the rental agreement.

### 14. CLEANLINESS & INTERIOR CARE

Please help us maintain the vehicle:
Do not throw rubbish inside the car.
Avoid spilling food or drinks.
Do not place muddy or excessively dirty items on the seats or interior.
Do not damage or remove any interior equipment.
Excessive dirt, stains, food spills, pet hair, smoke odor, or other unusual mess may result in an additional cleaning charge.

### 15. VEHICLE KEYS & ACCESSORIES

The customer is responsible for the vehicle keys and supplied equipment during the rental period.
This may include:
Vehicle keys.
Spare tyre.
Jack and tools.
Vehicle documents.
Safety equipment.
Charging cables or accessories, if provided.
Lost, damaged, or misused items may be charged to the customer.

### 16. VEHICLE MODIFICATIONS

Customers must not:
Modify the vehicle.
Install accessories without permission.
Remove vehicle equipment.
Tamper with the engine or electrical systems.
Disconnect or interfere with safety or tracking equipment, where fitted.

### 17. PROHIBITED USE

The vehicle must not be used for:
Racing or competitions.
Drifting.
Reckless driving.
Off-road driving unless specifically permitted.
Towing another vehicle unless authorized.
Transporting illegal or prohibited goods.
Any unlawful activity.
Any use that may put the vehicle, passengers, or public at unreasonable risk.

### 18. BREAKDOWN & WARNING LIGHTS

If a warning light appears or the vehicle develops a mechanical problem:
Safely stop the vehicle when possible.
Do not continue driving if doing so could cause further damage or create a safety risk.
Contact the rental company immediately.
Do not arrange major repairs without contacting the rental company first, except where immediate action is reasonably necessary for safety.

### 19. PARKING & VEHICLE SECURITY

Park only in safe and legally permitted areas.
Lock the vehicle whenever it is unattended.
Do not leave valuables visible inside the vehicle.
Keep the keys secure.
Never leave the vehicle running and unattended.
The customer may be responsible for loss or damage caused by negligence or failure to properly secure the vehicle, subject to the rental agreement and applicable law.

### 20. PERSONAL BELONGINGS

Customers are responsible for their own belongings.
The rental company is not responsible for personal items that are lost, stolen, forgotten, or left inside the vehicle.

### 21. VEHICLE INSPECTION

Before starting the rental:
The vehicle should be inspected by the customer and rental company.
Existing damage should be recorded where possible.
Photos/videos of the vehicle may be taken before and after the rental for documentation.
The customer should report any previously unnoticed damage before using the vehicle.

### 22. DOCUMENTS & DRIVER ELIGIBILITY

The customer must provide valid documents required for renting and legally driving the vehicle.
The customer confirms that:
Their driving licence is valid and legally permits them to drive the vehicle.
The information provided during the rental process is accurate.
They will immediately inform the rental company if their eligibility to drive changes during the rental period.

### 23. TOLLS, PARKING & OTHER ROAD CHARGES

The customer is responsible for applicable tolls, parking fees, permits, or other charges arising from their use of the vehicle, unless specifically included in the rental price.

### 24. MILEAGE & DISTANCE LIMIT

Each rental day includes 300 km of free driving distance.
Total distance driven is calculated from the vehicle's odometer reading at handover and at return.
If the total distance driven exceeds 300 km × the number of rental days, the excess distance is charged at the vehicle's listed extra-km rate.
Rate examples: Maruti Swift ₹12/km · Honda City, Kia Sonet, Maruti Brezza, Maruti Grand Vitara ₹15/km · Volkswagen Virtus, Mahindra Thar ₹20/km.
The exact rate for the vehicle being rented is shown on its listing before booking.
Excess-km charges are calculated and settled at the end of the rental, the same way as other final charges.

### 25. RETURN OF THE VEHICLE

The vehicle must be returned:
At the agreed time and location.
With the agreed fuel level.
With all keys, documents, and accessories.
In a reasonable condition.
Without unauthorized modifications or damage.
The vehicle will be inspected upon return.
